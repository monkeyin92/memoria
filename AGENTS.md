## Child-facing audio and scripts

Never synthesize or add child-facing audio or content from draft scripts on your own. PRs that ship such material need 产品部 and 合规部 sign-off before merge. Complete the 「儿童可听内容（故事/台词/音频）」 section in `.github/pull_request_template.md`.
